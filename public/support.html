Need help or want to report something?
Email us at jpgsportsbiz@gmail.com and we will get back to you as soon as we can.

Reporting a wrong stat or a missing player

Spotted a player who is missing, or a stat or team list that looks wrong? Send us an email with:

  • The player's name
  • Which category you were playing (for example, Career Home Runs)
  • What looked wrong, and what it should be if you know

Reporting a bug

If something in the app isn't working, tell us what you were doing, which game mode you were in, and what device and iOS version you have. A screenshot helps a lot.

Questions and feedback

Ideas for new modes, categories or players? We read every message.
